Delete your Habitto account

Habitto for Android, by __DEVELOPER_NAME__

You can delete your account and all its data at any time. There are 2 ways.

Option 1: in the app (at once)

  1. Open Habitto, and sign in.
  2. Open You → Settings.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE, and tap Delete.

The app deletes your account on our server at once. Then it deletes the data on your phone, and opens as a guest. Your phone must be online.

Option 2: by email (if you removed the app)

  1. Send an email from the address of your Habitto account to __SUPPORT_EMAIL__.
  2. Use the subject Delete my Habitto account.

We delete the account in 7 days or less, and we reply when it is done. If you send the email from another address, we ask you to confirm that you own the account.

A deletion is permanent. We cannot restore a deleted account or its data. To use Habitto again, you make a new account.

What we delete

  • Your account: the email address, the password hash, and the data from Google sign-in.
  • All your app content: habits, habit logs, wants, want logs, identities, goals and notes.
  • The custom habit templates that you made.

What we keep

We keep no data that is connected to your account. Crash reports and usage events from Firebase are not connected to your account. Firebase deletes them automatically: crash reports after 90 days, and usage events at the level of a device after 2 months. See the privacy policy.

Without an account

If you used Habitto without an account, we have no data of yours. Remove the app, or open Android Settings → Apps → Habitto → Storage → Clear storage. This deletes all the data on your phone.
