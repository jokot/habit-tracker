A trade you make with yourself

Most habit apps count streaks. Habitto also counts the cost. The time you spend on autopilot comes out of the time you spent on purpose.

Pick who you want to be

Choose identities like Reader, Athlete, Builder or Saver. Each one suggests habits, and you can write your own.

Log habits, earn points

Each habit has a size for 1 point, for example 10 minutes of reading or 1 km of running. Log it from the app or from a home screen widget.

Spend points on wants

Wants are the things you do without thinking. Start a timer for TikTok or YouTube, and the points go down while it runs. At 0, the timer stops.

Streaks make scrolling cost more

Complete your daily targets, and your streak grows. A longer streak moves you up a tier. Each tier makes a point buy less screen time, so a good run is harder to waste.

Streak history

Every day you complete shows on the grid. Miss one day, and the streak freezes instead of breaking.

Small app, few surprises

Home screen widgets

See your balance and streak, and log a habit or a want with one tap, without opening the app.

Works offline

Everything saves on your phone first. Use it with no account at all, or sign in to sync between devices.

Reminders you choose

A daily reminder at your time, and a warning before a streak breaks. Turn each one on or off.
